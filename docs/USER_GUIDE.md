# BCR LeadBook: Quick User Guide

BCR LeadBook keeps your clients, meeting notes and follow-ups on your phone. It works without internet and needs no account.

## The five-minute meeting entry

1. Tap **Add Lead / Meeting** (big blue button on the Home screen).
2. Type the **company name**. That is the only required field. If a similar company is already saved you will be offered **Open it**; tap **This is a different company** if it is not the same business.
3. Tap **Add the person you met** (name is enough; phone and email can wait). Next time, just pick them from the list.
4. Choose a **lead quality** (Hot, Warm, Cold, Future Follow-Up) if you know it. Tap it again to un-choose. Optionally set the **target follow-up month**, for example November 2026.
5. Under **Insurance products** tap **Add Product** (or **Add Another Product**). Pick Medical, Motor, Property, Marine, Liability or Other. Add a policy label if the company has several of the same type, and the expiry date if you know it. If you do not know it, leave it: the app never guesses.
6. Type your notes in the three boxes:
   - **Meeting Summary & Minor Details**: what was discussed, requirements, objections, promises, agreed next steps, small details.
   - **Personal & Rapport Notes**: hobbies, family, coffee order, favourite team. These stay with the *person* and are shown again before every future call.
   - **Company Trivia & Context**: expansion plans, news, challenges. These stay with the *company*.
7. Under **Next action** type what you must do, tap a quick date (Mon-Fri, Tomorrow, In 3 days, Next week…) or pick one, and leave **Remind me** on if you want a notification (default 9:00 AM).
8. Tap **Save**. A green "Saved" message confirms it.

**Different person for the next call?** Switch off *Next call is with the same person* and choose (or add) the key contact, for example the decision-maker.

**Interrupted?** Everything you type is saved automatically as a draft. If you leave, the app asks whether to keep or discard it; a yellow card on the Home screen lets you resume.

## Before your next call

Open the company (Leads tab). The **Pre-call** tab shows, in order: who to call (with Call / WhatsApp / Email buttons), the next action, the last meeting summary, rapport reminders, company context, and upcoming policy expiries ("23 days left", "Expired", or "Expiry unknown").
Other tabs: **Notes** (full dated meeting history and all rapport notes), **People**, **Policies**.
Tap **Add Meeting** to record the next meeting. It adds a new dated entry and never overwrites the old ones. If the company still has open follow-ups, the app asks which ones this meeting took care of and marks them done.

The Call, WhatsApp and Email buttons only *open* your phone, WhatsApp or email app. Nothing is ever sent automatically. For WhatsApp, save numbers with the country code (for example +971 50 123 4567).

## Follow-ups

The **Follow-ups** tab has Today, Upcoming, Overdue and Completed lists. Tap the circle to mark a follow-up done (Undo is offered). The ⋮ menu lets you **Reschedule** (with quick dates), **Snooze until tomorrow**, **Snooze for a week**, edit or delete. Follow-ups linked to a person show their name with one-tap Call and WhatsApp. Changing, completing or deleting a follow-up updates its reminder automatically.
The first time you save a follow-up with a reminder, the phone asks for permission to show notifications. If you say no, the app still works and still lists what is due, you just will not get pop-ups.
Notifications say only "You have a follow-up due." so nothing private shows on a locked screen (you can change this in Settings).

## Finding leads

On the **Leads** tab, type in the search box. It looks in company names, locations, contact names, phone numbers (any format) and notes. **Filters** narrow by quality, target month/year, insurance line and follow-up status; **Sort** orders by next follow-up, latest update, company name or nearest policy expiry. The **Renewals** list (Home → Upcoming renewals → See all) can be filtered by insurance line (for example only Medical) and shows every policy separately, so ABC Trading's Medical (15 Mar 2027) and Motor (20 Jul 2027) appear as two rows.

## Fixing mistakes

- Deleted a company, contact, meeting or product by accident? Tap **Undo** in the message that appears (about 8 seconds).
- Same client saved twice? Open one of them → ⋮ → **Merge into another company…**. Everything moves across and nothing is lost.
- Wrong name or expiry date? Open the company and tap Edit (company menu ⋮, People tab, Policies tab).

## Protect your data (please read)

Your records live **only on this phone**. If the app is uninstalled or the phone is lost, broken or reset, they are gone.
Go to **Settings → Create backup** regularly (for example every Friday) and save the file somewhere safe: Google Drive, iCloud, or email it to yourself. To move to a new phone or recover, use **Settings → Restore from backup file**. This *replaces* everything on the phone, so the app asks you to confirm first.
Settings shows when you last backed up, and the Home screen reminds you if you have never backed up or it has been over 30 days. Before a restore the app offers to back up your current data first.
**Export to CSV** makes spreadsheet files (including a one-sheet **renewals** worklist with the contact's phone) for Excel or Google Sheets. Personal & rapport notes are left out unless you tick the box.
Backup and CSV files are not password-protected. Treat them as confidential.

## Explore with demo data (optional)

Settings → *Load demo data* adds five fictional companies. *Remove demo data* deletes only those.

## Troubleshooting

- *No reminder appeared*: check Settings → *Check notification permission*; also check the phone's own notification and battery settings for BCR LeadBook. Android may deliver a reminder a few minutes late to save battery.
- *Changed time zone*: open the app once and the reminders move to the new local time.
- *WhatsApp does not open*: the number probably lacks its country code.
